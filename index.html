05 · Validate and measure with Tesco

  • Agree one journey, an accountable business owner and baseline measures.
  • Check themes against source records and operational context.
  • Measure investigation time, resolution outcomes, repeat contacts and colleague adoption.
  • Keep consequential customer or operational decisions with a human.
Decision principle: signal volume is only one input. Validate customer impact, urgency, confidence in the evidence and feasibility before deciding what to do.
